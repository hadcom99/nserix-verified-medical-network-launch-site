About nSerix

Precision-engineered for Algeria's physicians

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 June 2025 — Last updated: 1 June 2025

1. Who we are

nSerix (the "Platform") is operated by nSerix SAS, registered in Algiers, Algeria. We build congress intelligence and verified physician directory services exclusively for Algeria's medical community. Data controller contact: privacy@nserix.dz.

2. Scope and applicable law

This policy applies to all users of nserix.dz and related nSerix mobile web experiences. We are bound by Law No. 18-07 of 10 June 2018 (Algerian Personal Data Protection Law) and all subsequent ANPDP regulations. No personal data is transferred outside Algerian territory.

3. Data we collect and why

  • Congress visitors (no account): We collect no personally identifiable data. Programme access is anonymous; session bookmarks are stored locally in your browser.
  • Physicians requesting early access: Professional email and declared specialty — used solely to send access invitations and product updates.
  • Verified Physicians: Ordre number or society-verified identity, institutional affiliation, and wilaya — used to generate a verified profile in the bilingual directory. Never sold or shared with third parties.
  • Congress organisers: Name, role, and organisational email — used to provide congress management access.

4. Sovereign cloud storage

All data is stored on infrastructure located exclusively in Algeria, operated by Algerian-registered cloud providers. nSerix holds no cross-border data transfer agreements and does not use any international analytics, advertising, or identity platforms that would transfer physician data outside Algeria.

5. Data retention

  • Early access requests: retained for 24 months from submission, then deleted.
  • Verified Physician profiles: retained while the profile is active. Physicians may request deletion at any time by emailing privacy@nserix.dz.
  • Congress session logs: anonymised and aggregated after 90 days; raw logs deleted.

6. Your rights under Algerian law

You have the right to access, correct, delete, and object to processing of your personal data. Submit requests to privacy@nserix.dz. We respond within 15 business days. You may also lodge a complaint with the ANPDP.

7. Cookies and local storage

nSerix uses browser local storage solely to persist session bookmarks and language preference — no tracking cookies, no advertising pixels, no third-party analytics scripts are loaded.

8. Security

Data in transit is encrypted via TLS 1.3. Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256. Access to physician records is restricted to nSerix engineers via role-based access control with audit logs.

9. Changes to this policy

Material changes will be communicated by email to registered users at least 14 days before taking effect. The current version is always available at nserix.dz/privacy.

10. Contact
